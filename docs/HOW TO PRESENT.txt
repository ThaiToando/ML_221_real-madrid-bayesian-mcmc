Does money buy wins at Real Madrid?
Do Thai Toan - ML 221 Applied Probability, Final Project

HOW TO OPEN
1. Unzip the whole folder first (right-click the zip > Extract All). Do not open files from inside the zip.
2. Open the folder and double-click index.html. Use Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge.
   (If it opens in another browser: right-click index.html > Open with > Chrome.)
3. No internet is needed. Everything (charts, videos, photos, fonts, maths) is inside this folder.
   Keep the folder together: index.html needs the "assets" and "vendor" folders next to it.

KEYS WHILE PRESENTING
  Right arrow / Space   next point or slide
  Left arrow            back
  Down arrow            extra views under a slide (shown as "2 more views")
  F                     full screen (Esc to leave)
  S                     speaker notes in a second window (with timer)
  O                     overview of all slides
  K                     restart the clock in the footer
  V                     play / pause the video on the slide
  [  and  ]             video back / forward 5 seconds
  Alt + click           zoom into part of the slide (Alt + click again to zoom out)
Charts: hover for numbers, drag to rotate the 3D ones, press the Play button on a chart to replay it.

OTHER FILES
  deck.pdf    the file to submit (static pictures of every slide)
  deck.pptx   PowerPoint copy (static, for backup)

BEFORE THE TALK
  Open the deck once on the presentation computer and click through every slide and its extras,
  so the browser has loaded the videos.
